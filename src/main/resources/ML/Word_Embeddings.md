Tokens are the basic text units (words, subwords) from tokenization,  while embeddings are dense numerical 
vectors representing those tokens, 
capturing their semantic meaning and relationships in a mathematical way for
LLMs to process. Tokens are like unique IDs (e.g., "cat" = 1234), whereas 
embeddings are rich, high-dimensional arrays of numbers (e.g., `[0.2, -0.1, 0.9, ...]`) 
that show "cat" is similar to "kitten" but different from "car".
